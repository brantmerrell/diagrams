      ## None

      94 diagrams in this repository do not correspond to any\
      software design pattern, and that is the correct outcome rather than\
      a gap to be filled.

      - **`tech/examples/**`** documents D2 language features — block\
      strings, grids, LaTeX, tooltips. The subject matter is syntax, not\
      architecture.
      - **`tech/hackerrank/**`** documents single-function algorithms.\
      Most are a loop and an accumulator; naming a pattern there would be\
      cargo-culting. (A handful do show sliding-window, two-pointer, or\
      greedy *algorithmic* techniques, which are not design patterns.)
      - **`tech/sd-notes/tools/rdbms*`, `NoSQL`, `vector_database`,\
      `communication`** are reference notes on storage and protocol\
      taxonomies, not structures with a pattern intent.
      - **`tech/dev/**`** debugging write-ups are narratives of a specific\
      failure and its fix.
      - The remaining sequence diagrams describe concrete call flows that\
      happen not to be shaped by a named pattern.

      Diagrams here link back to this page so the classification is\
      explicit and reviewable, rather than being an absence of evidence.
