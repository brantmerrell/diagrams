      ## Mediator

      Colleagues that would otherwise form an N×N mesh all talk to one\

      - `sd-notes/tools/connectors.d2` — the connector layer sits between\
      heterogeneous producers and consumers so neither side has to know\
      the other's protocol or address.

    }

    colleague_a: "ColleagueA" {class: obj_service}
    colleague_b: "ColleagueB" {class: obj_service}
    colleague_c: "ColleagueC" {class: obj_service}

    colleague_a -> mediator.notify {class: edge_request}
    colleague_b -> mediator.notify {class: edge_request}
    colleague_c -> mediator.notify {class: edge_request}
    mediator.notify -> colleague_a {class: edge_write}
    mediator.notify -> colleague_b {class: edge_write}
    mediator.notify -> colleague_c {class: edge_write}

    note: |md

      edges and mutual knowledge of each other. With it, each knows\
      exactly one collaborator and the coupling collapses from N² to N.
